Returns & Exchange Policy 14-day returns on defective products

You may request a return within 14 days of delivery if the product has a manufacturing defect or a genuine product issue. All requests are subject to inspection by our team.

Eligible when:

  • The request is made within 14 days of delivery.
  • The product has a confirmed defect or genuine issue.
  • Photos or a video of the issue can be provided if requested.
  • Original packaging and accessories are included, if applicable.

Not accepted when:

  • It's a change of mind or fragrance preference.
  • The product was used or opened in a way that affects resale condition.
  • Damage was caused by misuse, improper storage, or an accident.
  • The request comes after the 14-day period.

If a defect is confirmed and cannot be repaired, the product may be exchanged for the same item, subject to availability.
